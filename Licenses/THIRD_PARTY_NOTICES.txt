SpaceTrace 1.0 — Third-party notices
======================================

SpaceTrace software and the bundled HRTF/head data are separate works with separate terms. LICENSE.txt covers only Lanes Audio first-party material where no more specific license is stated. A head package keeps the license/notice that belongs to its source data.

1. IRCAM LISTEN 1050
---------------------
Source: IRC_1050_R_44100.sofa
Official source: https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/listen%20%28hrtf%29/IRC_1050_R_44100.sofa
Copyright: (c) 2014-2017 IRCAM. All Rights Reserved.
Attribution carried by SpaceTrace: IRCAM LISTEN HRTF Database; Olivier Warusfel and the IRCAM Room Acoustics Team.

The preserved IRCAM notice grants educational, research and commercial use, requires the copyright notice to accompany reproductions when present, requests acknowledgment in published research, and requests written acknowledgment for commercial-product use. SpaceTrace's .sthrtf is a converted runtime representation: 128-tap minimum-phase ear responses plus explicit per-ear delay derived from the official SOFA. No HRTF bytes from historical games are redistributed.

Full preserved notice: IRCAM_LISTEN_NOTICE.txt and Heads/IRCAM_1050/LICENSE.txt

Full preserved notice: MIT_KEMAR_NOTICE.txt and Heads/MIT_KEMAR_Normal/LICENSE.txt

3. SADIE II D1 / Neumann KU100
--------------------------------
Source: D1_44K_16bit_256tap_FIR_SOFA.sofa from SADIE II
Current archive DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12092466
Copyright: 2018 University of York.
License: Apache License 2.0.

The SADIE notice requires the original dataset to be referenced whenever used in original or modified form. SpaceTrace converts subject D1 coordinates/data to .sthrtf and supplies a separate global Tone Trace correction. The bundled head files carry a prominent SpaceTrace change notice while retaining the SADIE attribution and Apache 2.0 terms.

Associated publication: C. Armstrong, L. Thresh, D. T. Murphy, and G. C. Kearney, “A Perceptual Evaluation of Individual and Non-Individual HRTFs: A Case Study of the SADIE II Database,” Applied Sciences 8(11):2029, 2018.

Full license/notice: SADIE_II_LICENSE.txt and Heads/KU100_SADIE_D1/LICENSE.txt

4. TH Köln / Bernschütz FULL2DEG KU100
---------------------------------------
Source: HRIR_FULL2DEG.sofa
Source DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.3928297
Creator: Benjamin Bernschütz, Technische Hochschule Köln
License applied by SpaceTrace: CC BY-SA 3.0 Unported.

There is conflicting record-level metadata on the modern Zenodo page, but the preserved SOFA says “CC 3.0 BY-SA” and the author's publication for this compilation explicitly identifies CC BY-SA 3.0. SpaceTrace therefore follows the more specific source/publication evidence. The .sthrtf is an adaptation because it changes format/precision/coordinate representation. SpaceTrace also places the FULL2DEG-specific Tone Trace model, correction and generated correction documentation under CC BY-SA 3.0 as a conservative boundary. ShareAlike applies to those adaptations, not to unrelated SpaceTrace code or the other works merely collected beside them.

Attribution/change notice: FULL2DEG_CC_BY_SA_3_0.txt and Heads/KU100_FULL2DEG/LICENSE.txt

5. FABIAN HATO 0
-----------------
Source: FABIAN_HRIR_measured_HATO_0.sofa
Source record: https://depositonce.tu-berlin.de/handle/11303/6153
License: CC BY 4.0 International.

SpaceTrace converts the SOFA to .sthrtf and generates FABIAN-specific correction/calibration documentation. The source metadata's modelled substitutions below 200 Hz / below -64° elevation remain part of the source dataset's provenance and are disclosed.

Attribution/change notice: FABIAN_CC_BY_4_0.txt and Heads/FABIAN_HATO0/LICENSE.txt

6. JUCE 8.0.12
---------------
JUCE is a build/framework dependency with its own licensing terms. See JUCE_NOTICE.txt. A binary SpaceTrace release must be made under a JUCE license route that permits that distribution. SpaceTrace's first-party license does not relicense JUCE.

-------------------------------------
SpaceTrace's CLAP wrapper uses the pinned free-audio CLAP, clap-helpers and clap-juce-extensions projects. Their MIT-style license texts are included as CLAP_LICENSE.txt, CLAP_HELPERS_LICENSE.txt and CLAP_JUCE_EXTENSIONS_LICENSE.txt.
