SpaceTrace changes:
- converted the source SOFA HRIR samples to the SpaceTrace .sthrtf runtime format;
- normalized source coordinates to SpaceTrace's canonical convention;
- stores runtime samples as float32;
- generated a head-specific Tone Trace tonal correction, correction model/plots/descriptions, level trim and front-center calibration for SpaceTrace.

The source metadata notes that HRIRs below 200 Hz and elevations below -64° contain numerically modelled substitutions made by the FABIAN dataset authors. SpaceTrace does not present those substitutions as its own measurements.
