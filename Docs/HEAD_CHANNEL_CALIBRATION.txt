SpaceTrace head channel calibration
==================================

Why this exists
---------------
A dataset can label a measurement as 0 degrees / front-center while the measured
left and right broadband levels are not perfectly equal. SpaceTrace measures that
condition instead of assuming every nominal 0-degree HRIR is centered.

The calibration is a pair of fixed equal-and-opposite gains stored in manifest.json.
It does not modify head.sthrtf or correction.wav. Equal-and-opposite dB gains preserve
the geometric mean of the gains while centering the nominal front response.
They do not preserve total stereo energy exactly; overall head trim remains a
separate calibration. Stereo Pair source-leg balance is also separate from these
output-ear gains.

Method
------
- Use the native head's real 0-degree azimuth / 0-degree elevation measurement.
- Measure each raw ear with pink weighting over 80 Hz-16 kHz.
- Compute L-R imbalance in dB.
- Apply half the difference in opposite directions: the quieter side is raised and
  the hotter side is lowered by the same dB amount.
- Reject unexpectedly large automatic corrections so coordinate/dataset problems are
  investigated rather than hidden.

Reproduce it with:
  python scripts/analyze_head_channels.py Heads/MyHead --write-manifest
